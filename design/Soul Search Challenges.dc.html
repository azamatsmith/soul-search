Examples: "Brown Week" (Thu Nov 12, 9:00 am – Sun Nov 15, 11:59 pm, Brown Souls worth +3) and a 4-hour "Thanksgiving Dash". Time-left copy: more than 24 hours left → "Ends Sun 11:59 pm · 2 days left"; under 24 hours → "Ends 6:00 pm · 3 hours left"; under an hour → "Ends 6:00 pm · 45 min left". Times are Pacific with lowercase am/pm. Assumption to confirm: a challenge pauses the weekly score while it runs (the action items say sightings count toward the challenge "instead of their week").

Admin: set up a challengeFrom Admin tools → Challenges. Exact start and end times, in Pacific time, so a challenge can last a few hours or several days. Rule overrides start from the weekly rules; an admin can change any value or add a bonus for one category.
